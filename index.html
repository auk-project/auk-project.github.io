Auk: An Open-Source Foundational Model for Speech Generation and Editing

One model for every speech task

Auk is a 1.5B-parameter foundational model that answers a natural-language instruction with edited or generated audio. A multimodal language model reads the instruction alongside optional reference audio; a 50 Hz VAE supplies acoustic latents; a hybrid transformer fuses both streams under a flow-matching objective.

<Auk logo>

The same breadth in four steps

Auk-Flash is distilled from Auk in two stages: a trajectory-level consistency initialization hands a few-step student over to a task-routing decoupled DMD objective, then supervises separation with clean-prediction regression. The result keeps the base model's breadth at 4 steps, with no CFG at inference.

Architecture

MLLM semantic condition

Qwen2.5-Omni encodes the instruction jointly with the optional reference audio. A learnable weighted sum of layer-wise hidden states is layer-normalized to form the semantic condition.

VAE acoustic latents

A flow-augmented 24 kHz VAE encodes audio to 50 Hz, 64-dim latents.

Hybrid transformer

30 layers, 1.5B parameters. The first 10 are dual-stream MMDiT blocks with RoPE and stream-specific residuals. The remaining 20 are single-stream DiT blocks over the concatenated sequence.

Pre-training scale

3.03B instruction–audio instances spanning 1.95M hours. 600k updates on 256 H20 GPUs, 32-sample micro-batches.
